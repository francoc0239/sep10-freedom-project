## What have I been doing?
### Presentations
Ever since the last blog entry, I've presented my final project to my classmates and my teacher. Over the past few weeks, hes been telling us to go present into the class and eventually, we would all have to present our work to the whole school by using elevator pitches. But before presenting to the whole school, we had to present to our class. For our class presentations, we had to make slides in order to show our project, our code, and our takeaways. Here are my [slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1mVLeSU44HhCN0NWGd-DVuCBHwAwHky5196HAwZ-r7zQ/edit?slide=id.g3e12c0c3639_0_0#slide=id.g3e12c0c3639_0_0) as an example. While making my slides for my presentation, It was hard trying to figure out how I wanted to present my own project. I eventually decied to do a demo of my project and show off some of my code as well as some takeaways like **Always be prepared for mistakes** and **to always be determined**. But presenting my sides to the class was the hardest part. I had to make a plan in order to get my viewer to get hooked and to know what i had to say. So i made a [plan](https://github.com/francoc0239/sep10-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/presentation.md) and tried to stick to it while presenting. Before and while presenting, I was very worried of messing up and looking bad infront of the class. But while and after presenting, I eventually realized that I should have been more relaxed. This is because while presenting, it felt less stressful, but in my head I was still worried. After the presentation, I felt like I should have been more confident because I know most of my classmates. I realized that next time I present, I should have more confident.
